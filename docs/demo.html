ListKit

Photograph a thing on your phone, write the listing on your laptop, paste it into Facebook Marketplace or Kijiji. Click through the four steps below.

This is a walkthrough, not the app. Every figure and line of text here is one worked example — an IKEA Billy bookcase — baked into the page. Nothing is analysed, no photos are uploaded, and the prices shown are illustrative rather than real listings. The working app runs on your own laptop; this page just shows you the shape of it.
Phone · same wifi

What the real app does that this page cannot

  • Strips the GPS coordinates out of your photos before they go anywhere.
  • Reads the actual photos to identify the item, its condition and its flaws.
  • Searches the live web for what comparable ones are being asked for.
  • Keeps the photos on your laptop, ready to drag into the upload box.

Why there is no hosted version

It needs a server — to keep the API key out of the browser, to store photos, and to receive the phone's upload. GitHub Pages serves static files only, so this walkthrough is the most that can live here. The app itself runs on your own laptop, which also keeps the photos in the house.
